Payment FAQ

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, there are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time through your dashboard or by reaching out to support.
What payment methods are accepted?
Through Razorpay, we accept all major Credit/Debit Cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe), NetBanking, and popular mobile wallets.
Will I get an invoice for my payment?
Absolutely. After each successful payment, an automated GST-compliant invoice will be sent to your registered email address.
Do you store my credit card information?
No. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay. We never store or even see your sensitive card details.
